# THE "NOT DOING" LIST

> Risk O-02: scope creep is the documented #1 killer of this exact project type.
> The concept began with 14 modules. Every customer will request a 15th.
> **Reviewed monthly. Nothing leaves this list without MASTER AARKASON's explicit order.**

## Currently NOT building

| Item | Why not | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|
| **3D visualisation (Three.js)** | 4–8 months of the only engineer; no customer's purchase decision depends on a rotatable cube; hardest thing to run on a site-grade Android | Pavement/FEM module, where geometry carries real information |
| **Flutter multi-platform** | 6 platforms = 6× surface area for 1 engineer | Never for v1. Web-first, always |
| **Native mobile apps** | App-install is a major Indian SMB drop-off point | PWA covers this |
| **BIM / IFC integration** | Enormous scope; our buyers (precast, labs) don't use BIM | Enterprise contractor segment, Stage 6+ |
| **Supplier marketplace** | Two-sided marketplace = two cold starts | Never as a startup move |
| **Blockchain** | The QR provenance record needs immutability, not a chain | Never |
| **ERP features** (payroll, attendance, billing) | Powerplay/Site Setu own this; not our fight | Never |
| **Structural design module** | Liability catastrophe; requires a sealed engineer | Not before incorporation + PI insurance + licensed co-signer |
| **TensorFlow / PyTorch** | Deep learning is wrong for ~200 rows; ~2 GB of dependencies | >10,000 validated records |
| **AWS / paid cloud** | Pay when customers pay | First revenue |
| **Fundraising** | Pre-revenue AI valuations are hostile in 2026 | 10 paying customers |

## The rule
Anything not in the **current stage's plan** goes to `docs/PARKING_LOT.md`, not into the build.
