# Patent-approval deck: technical QA notes

These points were explicitly controlled in the final presentation:

1. **Geopolymer terminology:** the tested patent embodiment is an OPC–GGBS concrete containing SFA and hooked steel fibres; it is **not** a geopolymer/alkali-activated concrete. Geopolymer efficiency is included only as published sustainability background.
2. **Density basis:** the manuscript alternates between wet/fresh and dry density. ACI 213R/ASTM C567 equilibrium-density benchmarks are shown as reference lines, not as a compliance declaration. ASTM C567 verification is still required.
3. **Extra-water protocol:** the narrative and final mix table conflict about compensation water. The disputed quantity is excluded from the proposed claim window until the laboratory record is reconciled.
4. **Impact-count variability:** error bars use sample standard deviations transcribed from the uploaded three-trial datasets:
   - N1 SD: NWC 0.58; LWC00 0.58; LWC05 0.58; LWC10 2.00; LWC15 4.36 blows.
   - N2 SD: NWC 0.58; LWC00 0.58; LWC05 3.61; LWC10 4.04; LWC15 31.19 blows.
5. **Impact acceptance criteria:** ACI 544.2R supplies a repeated-impact test procedure but no universal N1/N2/E2 qualifying threshold. No unsupported codal pass line was added to the impact graph.
6. **Barrier-scale inference:** one NWC and one LWC15 barrier were tested. The 2.0× failure-energy and 3.01× deformation observations are preliminary and do not support confidence intervals or a certified product guarantee.
7. **Occupant safety:** containment, redirection, ASI and THIV were not measured. Lower force transmission remains a mechanism-based hypothesis pending full-scale vehicle testing.
8. **Cross-study comparisons:** literature comparisons are directional because specimen dimensions and impact protocols differ. The final claims slide avoids direct cross-paper superiority claims.
9. **Proposed range:** the LWC05–LWC15 window is limited to three tested embodiments and is labelled as a technical evidence window—not final legal claim language.
10. **References:** unpublished manuscript and internal disclosure references were removed. Published papers, proceedings, standards and official datasets remain; DOI identifiers are included where available.
